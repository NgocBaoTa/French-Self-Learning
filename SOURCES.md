## Content policy

Use the sources for topic selection, level calibration, sequencing and exercise ideas. Store original examples and questions in the app. Do not copy textbook paragraphs, stories, answer keys or large passages into the product.

## Proposed curriculum map

### A1 foundation

- Greetings, introductions and personal information
- Family, daily routine, time and basic plans
- Shopping, food, transport and asking for directions
- Articles, gender/number, subject pronouns and present tense
- Negation, simple questions, possessives and common prepositions

### A2 expansion

- Housing, health, travel, work and practical administration
- Describing past events and personal experiences
- Near future and simple future intentions
- Passé composé versus imparfait in guided narratives
- Comparisons, object pronouns, quantity and cause/consequence

### B1 bridge

- Opinions, explanations, advice and discussion strategies
- Work, study, media, society and life in Canada/francophone contexts
- Connectors, relative pronouns, reported speech and narrative control
- Vocabulary by collocation and register, not isolated translation only
